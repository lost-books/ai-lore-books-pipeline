# TEXT GENERATION prompt

Write the complete book manuscript described below. Return the manuscript itself in Markdown, not an outline, treatment, editorial report, or explanation of the process.

Use the exact title as the Markdown H1. Aim near the requested length without padding. If a chapter count is supplied, use exactly that many Markdown H2 chapters. If mystery is the only selected mode and no count is supplied, use eight H2 chapters. Otherwise choose the structure that best serves the premise rather than imposing chapters or fragments.

Compose the finished manuscript directly in one pass. Combine invention, selective transformation, pruning, and continuity judgment while writing. Do not simulate agents, scoring passes, Wreckers, or independent conversations. Do not produce comparison drafts or an automatic later smoothing pass.

Develop a particular being, object, practice, place, or material process whose consequences can unfold. Use purposeful activity, specific observations, decisions, resistance, and consequences. Strangeness must alter what can happen, what someone must do, or what is lost. Preserve productive contradictions and incomplete understanding.

Avoid decorative random weirdness, generic ominous prose, interchangeable surreal images, repeated impossible corridors, arbitrary talking objects, and a strange final sentence mechanically attached to every paragraph. Do not default to domestic anecdote, corporate bureaucracy, a handbook, a named investigator, or a stock discovery preface. Never mention assistant identity, chat instructions, TEXT GENERATION, UNDERPASS, Pressworks, or internal process language inside the fiction.

For every new proper name, apply the Namer skill when available. In a chat without installed skills, use these naming rules: first determine whether the entity needs a name at all. Preserve established names exactly unless renaming is explicitly requested. Infer the relevant naming system from existing names and the world's cultural, linguistic, historical, geographic, and social evidence; distinguish multiple systems rather than blending them. If evidence is weak, prefer ordinary, low-ornament forms and treat early inventions as provisional. Internally compare several candidates across entity categories for unsupported genre patterns, concentrated sounds or morphology, imitation of recent inventions, and reader confusion. Shared patterns need an in-world reason. Do not use semantic symbolism or a blacklist as the primary naming mechanism. Choose the name best supported by the manuscript and put only the chosen name in context, without exposing candidate lists or analysis.

Blend the selected modes throughout one manuscript:

- Recovered: imply original use, traces of handling, missing shared knowledge, and incomplete understanding. Let the premise determine the document form. Discovery prefaces and fragments are optional. Call fragments `fragments`, never `leaves`.
- Procedural: develop practical tasks whose rules, costs, and consequences escalate. Keep the action particular and readable without forcing corporate administration.
- Mystery: use a compulsively readable, unreliable obsessive voice in which evidence, speculation, memory, and certainty collide. Each major discovery should make the current explanation inadequate and force a stranger replacement theory. Absorb objections rather than offering balanced analysis. Escalate the argument, not merely physical danger.
- Mythology: develop cultural memory, powers, ritual, weather, names, migration, and transformation through concrete activity and cost. Keep cosmology incomplete.

When sources are supplied, use them as pressure, vocabulary, and world knowledge rather than plot templates. Explicit premise corrections override them. Never name a source book inside the manuscript merely because it supplied research. Do not copy source sentences. Express source lore obliquely through events, beliefs, materials, imagery, and conflicting interpretations. If requested sources are unavailable in this chat, state that limitation outside the manuscript and do not claim source grounding.

When a finished style example is supplied, follow its motion, density, and voice without borrowing events, entities, or sentence-level language.

Respect any request for no ending. Stop while the document's activity remains underway, without a concluding lesson, retrospective summary, solved mechanism, last-minute twist, or cosmetic ellipsis. Otherwise end in a form appropriate to the book without explaining away the central unknown.

After the manuscript, report the actual word count and selected modes. Label it ready for review rather than approved. Do not generate images.
