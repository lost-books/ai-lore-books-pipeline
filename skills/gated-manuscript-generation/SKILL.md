# Gated Manuscript Generation

Prevent weak creative commitments before writing a complete book. Adapt the internal preflight approach of Gated Visual Ecology to prose without turning a manuscript into a population of unrelated concepts.

The workflow is:

`brief + relevant sources + prior steering → compact internal approach → preflight gates → one complete manuscript → focused checks → review`

## Load the current foundation

Read the installed [Manuscript Generation skill](../manuscript-generation/SKILL.md) before each new manuscript task. Its current inputs, defaults, source grounding, modes, structure, naming, ending, and delivery requirements apply. This skill adds preflight gates to that workflow; it does not replace the foundation with a frozen copy. If the dependency cannot be found, report the missing dependency rather than silently substituting an older workflow.

Read and apply [Namer](../namer/SKILL.md) before inventing any proper name, including names introduced during planning. The current foundation's Namer handoff applies throughout composition. Preserve established names exactly; accept a decision that an entity needs no name. Do not defer naming-system work until after drafting.

User instructions and current corrections take precedence over defaults. Do not require outline approval or an additional questionnaire when the brief is sufficient. Keep the ordinary manuscript-generation skill available for requests that do not call for this gated workflow.

## Establish a compact approach

Collect the title, premise, length, modes, source authority, explicit exclusions, structure, and ending limits. For revisions, identify the exact draft being revised and preserve it. Read available accepted and rejected work and steering relevant to this book; do not invent feedback or generalize one book's preferences into universal bans.

Internally establish only what is needed to begin a strong manuscript:

- The premise commitments and established facts that must survive.
- The particular being, practice, object, place, or material process through which those commitments become active.
- The document form and voice, with a reason they serve this premise.
- A few possible developments in which activity or evidence changes subsequent possibilities, costs, beliefs, or obligations.
- What remains unknown and what the requested stopping condition permits.
- Existing naming evidence, distinct naming systems where supported, and whether any new names are necessary.
- Relevant prior failures or repeated defaults to avoid.

These are provisional commitments, not a mandatory chapter outline. Leave room for discoveries during composition. Do not write comparison manuscripts, sample chapters, or a roster of alternative treatments unless requested.

## Preflight gates

Evaluate the proposed approach before full composition. Fix concrete weaknesses in the approach, not by spending a complete draft on a foreseeable failure. Keep the evaluation internal; do not expose candidate lists or reasoning transcripts.

### Premise and grounding

The approach must develop this brief's distinctive subject rather than a nearby familiar story. Relevant source passages must support the properties being used; distinguish established lore from speculation and new invention. Preserve explicit corrections over conflicting source assumptions. Do not shrink an ambitious premise into a generic domestic anecdote, investigator plot, or administrative packet merely because that form is easy to generate.

### Consequential strangeness

Unusual elements must change what can happen, what must be done, what can be believed, or what is lost. Apply a removal check: if an oddity can disappear without affecting the activity or implications, reconsider its function. Ordinary details and quiet passages need not each carry a supernatural effect.

### Form and voice necessity

The form must enable the intended reading experience. Recovered does not automatically mean archival fragments; procedural does not automatically mean bureaucracy; mythology does not require a cosmology lecture. For mystery mode, preserve the foundation's unstable obsessive argument rather than substituting a conventional investigation. Blend requested modes within one work.

### Development and creative distance

Planned developments must change the situation or interpretation, not repeat the same device with different objects. Watch for repeated impossible measurements, ominous notices, uncanny paragraph endings, and interchangeable discoveries. Recurring motifs are valid when their use or consequence develops. Do not force every section to introduce a new mechanism.

### Specificity and meaningful risk

Replace vague aims such as eerie, surreal, or mysterious with particular activities, observations, resistance, or consequences. Permit a non-obvious implication that grows from the premise and changes the work materially. Do not maximize novelty or add arbitrary weirdness to satisfy a gate. A direct treatment can pass when it is distinctive and consequential.

### Coherence and unresolved mystery

Make local action legible enough to follow while leaving ultimate causes unresolved where appropriate. Preserve productive contradictions; distinguish them from accidental continuity errors. Do not explain the central unknown merely to make the plan internally tidy. Avoid dictating a fixed reveal that closes off stronger discoveries during composition.

### Whole-manuscript balance

Check the work's proposed movement as a whole for unintentional concentration in one rhetorical gesture, emotional temperature, scale, or kind of evidence. Vary where it serves this book. Deliberate austerity, a narrow setting, or a sustained voice can be correct. Do not import image-round diversity quotas, medium requirements, or cover-candidate rules into prose.

### Naming and prior drift

Confirm that Namer has been applied to any proposed new proper names. Compare naming evidence across entity categories, preserve established spellings, and avoid letting the first invention dictate every later name. Check the approach against actual prior steering and rejected strategies, not just rejected words. No name blacklist or generic fantasy register substitutes for manuscript-specific evidence.

### Delivery constraints

Ensure the approach can honor the requested title, approximate length, exact chapter count when supplied, chosen modes, and ending limits. A no-ending request means ongoing activity rather than an explanatory conclusion or a cosmetic cliffhanger.

## Close preflight and compose

Proceed when there is a workable approach with no known conflict with the brief and no identified weakness that would undermine the whole draft. Gates are decision aids, not numerical scores or guarantees of literary quality. A simple brief may require very little planning.

Do not loop indefinitely in pursuit of certainty. If a weakness persists, simplify the unsupported commitment. Ask only when a material conflict or missing required input cannot be resolved from context. Do not use aesthetic uncertainty as a reason to demand approval.

When retaining a production record, save only a compact decision summary: chosen form, governing pressure, constraints, unresolved factual limitations, and any intentional concentration. Do not save hidden deliberation or a gate-by-gate thought transcript. Preflight completion means ready to draft, not user-approved.

Write one complete manuscript using the current foundation. Continue applying Namer whenever a new name becomes necessary. Allow discoveries to change the provisional approach while preserving the brief. Recheck only affected commitments when the direction changes; do not restart preflight for every paragraph. Do not simulate agents, Wreckers, or scoring panels.

## Check, deliver, and learn

Apply the foundation's delivery checks to the actual manuscript, including actual word count, structure, naming continuity, source-title leakage, and ending. Also check whether the draft realizes consequential development and avoids the repetitions identified in preflight. Planning alone is not evidence that the prose succeeds.

Correct identifiable local failures while preserving strong prose and productive contradictions. Do not automatically smooth, rewrite the whole manuscript, or generate another version for comparison. If a fundamental brief requirement remains unmet, report the specific limitation rather than calling the draft ready.

Save the complete Markdown manuscript and report its actual word count and modes outside the fiction. Label it ready for review, never approved. Do not generate images, map illustrations, assemble documents, or write product descriptions without the corresponding request or approval.

Treat reactions to the actual draft as the primary steering signal. Retain book-specific accepted choices and rejected strategies for a requested revision. Create a sibling version when revising; preserve prior drafts and established names unless the request changes them. Stop after delivery for review.
